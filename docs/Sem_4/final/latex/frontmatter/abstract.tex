\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}

Public social media is now a primary real-time channel for retail customer
voice.  DataReportal (2024) reports about 5.04 billion active social users,
Reddit's S-1 filing (Feb 2024) discloses $\sim$267 million weekly active
uniques, and Sprout Social's 2024 Index finds 51\% of consumers expect a
same-day brand response on social.  At the same time, the World Economic
Forum (2024) estimates that fake online reviews influence approximately
USD~152 billion in purchases each year.  Any analytical layer that consumes
this signal must therefore filter noise and low-credibility content
explicitly.

This dissertation designs, implements, and evaluates \emph{Retail Sentiment
Intelligence} (RSI), a working end-to-end prototype that ingests public Reddit
posts from a curated set of 32 retail-relevant communities, applies sentiment
classification with a fine-tuned \textbf{ModernBERT} model trained through a
three-stage curriculum, applies aspect-based opinion mining with a zero-shot
\textbf{DeBERTa-v3 NLI} classifier over a fixed eight-aspect retail taxonomy
(pricing, product quality, customer service, store experience, online/app,
delivery/pickup, returns, account/login), captions image attachments with
\textbf{Gemma 3 4B} (Ollama) using a multi-pass prompt pipeline that reduces
hallucination from 50\% to 0\% and lifts text extraction from 25\% to 75\%,
and assigns each post a 0--1 trust score derived from account-metadata
heuristics combined with an LLM-graded credibility scorer.  Trust-filtered,
aspect-tagged results are aggregated and presented in a React/FastAPI
dashboard with a human-in-the-loop Review \& Validate workflow, a smart
reply composer (rule + LLM cascade), a learning loop that captures analyst
corrections for future retraining, and a priority-negatives feed (P1 / P2)
ranked by \textsf{trust $\times$ confidence}.

Evaluation on a 5-fold out-of-fold cross-validation of the labelled sample
raises sentiment macro-F1 from 0.6272 (baseline) to \textbf{0.7642} overall and
from 0.2778 to \textbf{1.0000} on long posts.  The prototype uses only public
data and is not deployed inside Walmart systems.

\vspace{10pt}
\noindent\textbf{Key Words:} sentiment analysis; ModernBERT; aspect-based
opinion mining; zero-shot NLI; vision-language models; trust scoring; Reddit;
retail feedback.
